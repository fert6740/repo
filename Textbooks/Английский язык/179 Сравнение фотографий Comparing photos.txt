При сравнении фотографий следует придерживаться следующего плана.

1. Вступление

Начать монологическое высказывание поможет вступительная фраза:

I would like to compare and contrast the two photos.

Далее следует дать краткое описание каждой из них: кто или что запечатлено на фотографии, что и где происходит.

Теперь можно переходить к сравнению, то есть описанию сходств и различий.

2. Сходства

Для описания схожих характеристик мы можем использовать фразы:

There are some / few / many similarities…

There are more similarities than differences.

As for the similarities…

One of the few similarities is…

Both photos depict…

What the pictures have in common is…

They are almost the same in terms of…

The most striking / most obvious / most noticeable similarity is…

3. Различия

Описать различия нам помогут фразы:

As for the differences between these two pictures…

The photos are completely / totally different because…

The only difference is…

There is a slight / tiny difference between…

The first picture shows…, while the second…

The first picture depicts…, whereas the other picture…

The second picture doesn’t show … as clearly as the first picture does.

4. Заключение

В заключении необходимо рассказать о впечатлении, которое произвели на вас фотографии, а также какое изображение вам понравилось больше и почему. Иногда задание предполагает высказать своё предпочтение относительно ситуаций, запечатлённых на фотографии. В этом помогут выражения:

I’d personally prefer…

As for me, …

I would choose…

This option / event / situation … attracts me more.

I would like to compare and contrast the two photos.

In photo 1 we can see a group of people hiking in the mountains. Picture 2 shows a man deep into reading a book.

The most noticeable similarity is that both pictures show people doing leisure activities. And I suppose they enjoy the things they are doing. Another thing the pictures have in common is that both photos were taken outside and they have grass and mountains in the pictures.

As for the differences between these two pictures, I would mention the following: firstly, the first picture shows a group of people, while the second depicts a man who is spending his free time alone. He is involved in reading. We can suppose that the plot is gripping and nothing can distract the man from his activity. Secondly, the first picture doesn’t show feelings and emotions as clearly as the second picture does. I’ve also noticed that the weather in the 2 pictures is different. In the second picture it is sunny whereas in the first picture we can see a cloudy day. I suppose it may rain soon.

I like both pictures because of the special atmosphere. As for me, I would prefer to spend my weekends or holidays in a more active way. Though, reading in a park or in the countryside, looks attractive as well.
