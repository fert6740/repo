**Биография** — это описание жизни человека, сделанное другими людьми или им самим (автобиография).

Чтобы составить краткую биографию интересного нам человека, необходимо собрать информацию о нём и написать текст.

Как правило, в биографии упоминают следующие факты.

**1. Дату и место рождения**

He / she was born in…

**2. Где и как прошло детство, школьные годы**

His / her parents are / were…

They lived in…

He / she studied… / was interested in… / was fond of… / was keen on…

**3. Карьеру и общественную жизнь, награды и достижения**

He / she studied at … University.

He / she started working as…

He / she travelled to…

He / she was awarded with…

**4. Личную жизнь, интересы**

He / she married… in…

He / she worked on…

**5. Любопытные факты в жизни человека**

Если мы пишем биографию знаменитости, следует упомянуть, почему этот человек популярен.

He / she is popular with… / famous for…

Если человека уже нет в живых, следует упомянуть дату и место смерти.

He / she died in…

Начинать биографию следует с описания, чем занимается человек и чем он знаменит.

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, industrial designer, business magnate, and investor. He was the co-founder, chairman, and CEO of Apple.

Joanne Rowling is a British author.

[рисунок https://uploads-foxford-ru.ngcdn.ru/uploads/tinymce_file/file/111477/79b715c12881f9ce.png]

Dian Fossey was an animal expert.

She was born in San Francisco, California in 1932. Her parents divorced when she was six. Her mother remarried the following year, but Fossey’s stepfather never treated her as his own child. Dian turned to animals as a way to gain warm relationships. Her love for animals began with her first pet goldfish and continued throughout her entire life.

After school, Dian studied medicine at college and graduated in 1954. She worked at a children’s hospital in Louisville, Kentucky for several years. In 1963, she took a trip to eastern Africa, where she began to study the mountain gorilla in its natural habitat. On September 24, 1967, Fossey founded the Karisoke Research Center and made discoveries about gorillas. Dian is famous for the book about her relationships with gorillas, “Gorillas in the mist”. The book was filmed in 1988.

Dian was really fond of gorillas, she even had a favourite one, a young male called Digit.

Gorillas are often killed by poachers who sell their heads as decorations. Dian declared war on the poachers. On December 26th 1985 she was found murdered in her cabin. She was 53 years old.
